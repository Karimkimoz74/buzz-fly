1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They allow the site to remember information about your visit — such as your preferred language or login status — so that your next visit is easier and the site is more useful to you.

Beyond traditional cookies, we also use similar technologies such as local storage, session storage, and pixel tags. Throughout this policy, "cookies" refers collectively to all of these technologies.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We rely on cookies for a number of essential and optional purposes:

  • Keeping you signed in — so you don't have to log in on every page or every visit.
  • Remembering your preferences — currency, language, seat class, and display settings.
  • Securing your session — detecting suspicious activity and protecting your account from unauthorised access.
  • Completing bookings — maintaining your search and basket state while you move through the booking flow.
  • Measuring performance — understanding which pages load slowly or where users drop off so we can improve the experience.
  • Personalising content — showing destination recommendations and deal alerts relevant to your travel history.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Always Active

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function. They enable core features such as authentication, booking session management, and security. Without them, parts of the site cannot operate. You cannot disable essential cookies through our Cookie Settings, though you can block them in your browser — but this may break key functionality.

Analytics Cookies

Optional

We use analytics tools (including anonymised data from third-party providers) to understand how visitors interact with Buzz Fly — which pages are most visited, how users navigate between sections, and where errors occur. All data collected is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally. You can opt out at any time via Cookie Settings.

Preference Cookies

Optional

These cookies remember your personalisation choices — such as your preferred currency, language, cabin class, and home airport — so we can present a consistent experience across visits. Disabling them means your preferences will reset each time you return to the site.

Marketing Cookies

Requires Consent

With your explicit consent, we use marketing cookies to display relevant travel offers and promotions on Buzz Fly and across partner platforms. These cookies track your interactions with our ads to measure campaign effectiveness. We never share identifiable data with advertisers. You can withdraw consent at any time via Cookie Settings.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on Buzz Fly are placed by third-party services we integrate with. These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies which govern how they use data collected through their cookies:

Payment Providers

Stripe and PayPal may set cookies to detect fraud and process payments securely.

Analytics Platforms

Aggregated, anonymised usage data may be processed by analytics providers under data-processing agreements.

Mapping Services

Interactive maps on destination and airport pages may load cookies from map providers.

Social Sign-In

If you sign in via Google or Apple, those providers may set their own authentication cookies.

5. Your Controls

You have full control over non-essential cookies. Here are the ways you can manage them:

Cookie Settings panel — Use the "Manage Cookie Settings" button below to toggle optional cookie categories at any time.
Browser settings — Most browsers let you block or delete all cookies. Note that blocking essential cookies will affect site functionality.
Opt-out links — Where third-party analytics are used, you may opt out directly via the provider's own opt-out tool.
Withdraw consent — You can withdraw marketing cookie consent at any time; this will not affect any prior processing.
