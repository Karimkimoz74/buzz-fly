Cancellations

Understand refund timelines and manage travel voucher conversions.

Free Cancellation

Cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full refund, regardless of fare type.

Standard Fares

Cancellations made 7+ days before departure receive a full refund minus a $35 processing fee. Within 7 days, a travel voucher is issued instead.

Non-Refundable Fares

No cash refund. A travel voucher equal to the ticket value (minus fees) is issued and valid for 12 months.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Baggage

Track lost items or check allowance for international routes.

Carry-on

1 bag up to 10 kg (55 × 40 × 23 cm) — included in all fares.

Checked Baggage

Economy: 1 × 23 kg free. Business: 2 × 32 kg free. Extra bags charged at check-in.

Oversized / Special Items

Sports equipment, strollers, and musical instruments must be declared at booking. Fees vary by route.

Lost or Delayed Baggage

Report immediately at the airport desk or via My Trips within 24 hours of arrival.

Quick Answers

Yes — most fare types allow changes up to 24 hours before departure. Depending on your ticket class, a change fee or fare difference may apply. Elite members can make changes for free up to 2 hours before departure. Go to My Trips to see your specific options.

If your flight is delayed by more than 2 hours, you are entitled to meal vouchers at the airport. For delays over 5 hours you may request a full refund or a free rebooking on the next available flight. Notifications are sent automatically to your registered email and app. You can also track real-time status under My Trips.

Pet policies vary by hotel. When searching for hotels, look for the "Pet Friendly" filter to see only properties that allow pets. Each hotel listing shows its specific rules — including size limits, breed restrictions, and any per-night pet fee. Always confirm with the hotel directly before arrival, as policies can change.

Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of your cancellation request being approved. The amount is returned to your original payment method. Credit/debit card refunds may take an additional 5–7 business days to appear depending on your bank. Travel vouchers are issued instantly and can be used on your next booking.

You can add extra baggage before your flight through My Trips — simply select your booking and choose "Add Baggage." Adding baggage online is cheaper than paying at the airport. Fees vary by route and how far in advance you add the bag.

Online check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 1 hour before departure for most domestic flights, and 2 hours before for international flights. You can check in from My Trips and download your boarding pass directly to your device.

Travel requirements vary by destination and nationality. We recommend checking the official government travel advisory for your home country and your destination before booking. Entry requirements — including visas, vaccinations, and health declarations — are displayed on the flight details page when you search. Our support team can also assist with specific queries.
