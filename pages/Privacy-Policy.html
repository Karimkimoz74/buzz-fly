Privacy Policy

At Buzz Fly, your privacy is at the heart of everything we do. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — in plain language.

Last updated: January 1, 2024
Data We Collect How We Use It Sharing Cookies Your Rights Security Contact Us

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, as well as data generated when you use our services:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and password when you register.
  • Booking details — flight and hotel selections, passenger names, seat preferences, and meal choices.
  • Payment information — card type and last four digits (full card numbers are processed by our PCI-compliant payment partners and never stored on our servers).
  • Travel documents — passport numbers and expiry dates provided during check-in or for visa assistance, stored encrypted.
  • Usage data — pages visited, search queries, clicks, and device/browser information collected automatically to improve our services.
  • Location data — approximate location inferred from your IP address; precise location only if you grant permission in our mobile app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to deliver and improve our travel services:

  • Process bookings, issue e-tickets, and send itinerary confirmations.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • Personalise recommendations — destination ideas, deal alerts, and concierge suggestions — based on your travel history and preferences.
  • Send service-related notifications (flight delays, gate changes, check-in reminders) and, where you have opted in, promotional communications.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorised access, and other security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations such as aviation security regulations and financial reporting requirements.

3. Sharing Your Information

We never sell your personal data. We share it only in these limited circumstances:

  • Airlines & hotels — passenger and guest details required to fulfil your booking.
  • Payment processors — card data shared with PCI DSS-certified providers solely to complete transactions.
  • Service providers — cloud hosting, analytics, and customer support vendors who process data on our behalf under strict data-processing agreements.
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction with the same privacy protections intact.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how you use Buzz Fly:

Essential Cookies

Required for core functionality such as authentication and booking sessions. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand which features are most useful. You can opt out via Cookie Settings.

Preference Cookies

Remember your language, currency, and display settings across visits.

Marketing Cookies

Used to show relevant travel offers. Requires your explicit consent and can be withdrawn at any time.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights over your personal data:

Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Rectification — Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
Erasure — Request deletion of your data where we no longer have a legal basis to retain it.
Portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Objection — Opt out of processing for direct marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, visit your Account Settings or contact our Privacy Team directly.

6. How We Protect Your Data

We apply industry-standard safeguards to protect your information:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
  • Sensitive fields (passwords, travel document numbers) are encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Access to personal data is restricted on a strict need-to-know basis and protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • We conduct regular third-party security audits and penetration tests.
  • In the event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by applicable law.
